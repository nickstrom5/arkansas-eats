Arkansas barbecue

8 places for Arkansas barbecue, from the Delta's pork sandwiches to Hot Springs ribs. Each was checked against a 2025 or 2026 source in fall 2026.

From the Delta to the Ozarks

Jones Bar-B-Q Diner in Marianna serves pork barbecue on white bread, cash only; the Jones family has run it since the 1910s, and in 2012 it became Arkansas's first James Beard honoree. Craig's in De Valls Bluff has served pork sandwiches since 1947, the Dixie Pig in Blytheville its "pig sandwiches" since 1923, and McClard's in Hot Springs, founded in 1928, pairs its barbecue with "The Spread," a tamale plate.

The list at a glance

One each in Blytheville, Carlisle, De Valls Bluff, Fayetteville, Fort Smith, Hot Springs, Johnson and Marianna.

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (Arkansas Tourism's own pages, the Arkansas Food Hall of Fame or a dated local story) and its name matched a place in its town. No current source, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.
