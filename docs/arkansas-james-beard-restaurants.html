What counts as an honor here

James Beard Foundation honors, reported as facts rather than scores. The Foundation named Jones Bar-B-Q Diner in Marianna an America's Classic in 2012, Arkansas's first James Beard award, and Lassis Inn in Little Rock one in 2020. Matthew Cooper of Conifer and Rafael Rios of Yeyo's, both in Bentonville, were Best Chef: South finalists in 2025; Jason Paul of Heirloom in Rogers was one in 2026, when Onyx Coffee Lab was a finalist for Outstanding Bar. No MICHELIN Guide covers Arkansas. The Arkansas Food Hall of Fame has its own page.
