Arkansas's oldest restaurants

Restaurants and bars founded in 1960 or before and still serving: 7 places in all, oldest first. The oldest, The Ohio Club in Hot Springs, goes back to 1905.

Ask for early accessThe free Arkansas Eats app for iPhone and iPad is on its way to the App Store.

Which year counts

We count from the year the business opened, as a dated 2026 source gives it. Some have moved since, and a building's age isn't the business's.

Other guides: Cheese Dip Trail, Barbecue, Catfish houses, Dairy bars & drive-ins, Homemade pie, Food Hall of Fame, James Beard honors. City pages: Little Rock, Fayetteville.

Oldest first

  1. The Ohio Club

    336 Central Ave, Hot Springs

    Food Hall of Fame Since 1905

    Arkansas's oldest bar, since 1905; an Arkansas Food Hall of Fame inductee (2025).

  2. Ozark Cafe

    107 E Court St, Jasper

    Food Hall of Fame Since 1909

    Burgers, chicken-fried steak and chocolate gravy on the square since 1909; an Arkansas Food Hall of Fame inductee (2023).

  3. The Dixie Pig

    701 N 6th St, Blytheville

    Barbecue Since 1923

    "Pig sandwiches" since 1923; a 2026 Arkansas Food Hall of Fame finalist.

  4. McClard's Bar-B-Q

    505 Albert Pike Rd, Hot Springs

    BarbecueFood Hall of Fame Since 1928

    Barbecue and "The Spread" tamale plate; founded 1928.

  5. Neal's Cafe

    806 N Thompson St, Springdale

    Homemade pieFood Hall of Fame Since 1944

    Fried chicken and pies since 1944; an Arkansas Food Hall of Fame inductee (2022).

  6. Craig's Bar-B-Q

    15 W Walnut St, De Valls Bluff

    BarbecueFood Hall of Fame Since 1947

    Pork barbecue sandwiches since 1947; an Arkansas Food Hall of Fame inductee (2019).

  7. Venesian Inn

    582 W Henri De Tonti Blvd, Tontitown

    Food Hall of Fame Since 1947

    Spaghetti and fried chicken since 1947; an Arkansas Food Hall of Fame inductee (2018).
