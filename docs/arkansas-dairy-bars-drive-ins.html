Four in the Hall of Fame

The Arkansas Food Hall of Fame has inducted Ed Walker's Drive-In in Fort Smith (2020), Feltner's Whatta-Burger in Russellville (2021), Dairy King in Portia (2022) and Kream Kastle in Blytheville (2023).

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Benton (3), Fort Smith (2), Lincoln (1), Siloam Springs (1), Monticello (1), Helena-West Helena (1), Mountainburg (1), Portia (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (Arkansas Tourism's own pages, the Arkansas Food Hall of Fame or a dated local story) and its name matched a place in its town. No current source, and it stayed off. Review sites played no part, and places run by county and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.

On your phone, Arkansas Eats orders this list by how far each place is from you, and a tap opens Apple Maps' live card with hours, photos and directions.
