# Arkansas Eats: name, icon and palette (final: Nick's decisions, 2026-11-07)

## Name
- **Arkansas Eats** (App Store name "Arkansas Eats: Restaurants", Nick's choice 2026-11-07, 26 characters; "Arkansas Eats: Restaurant Guide" is 31, over the
  30 limit; Home Screen "Arkansas Eats", 13). A web search (2026-11-06) found no app called "Arkansas Eats"; near ones are the
  Division of Arkansas Heritage's "Arkansas Food Hall of Fame" app (check-ins and stamps), OnlyPlates (dish ratings in Northwest
  Arkansas), a "Sara's Weeknight Meals" TV episode titled "Arkansas Eats", and (per the prompt) a small Facebook page; keep clear of
  the Arkansas Times' "Eat Arkansas".
- Alternatives from the prompt: **Arkansas Table**; **Bear State Eats** (an old state nickname).
- Avoided: "The Natural State" (Arkansas Tourism Development Foundation's registered service mark, Reg. 3152686), "Diamond State"
  (Delaware's), Razorback, Hog and "Woo Pig" wording, and "Arkie". The Home header's eyebrow is "FROM THE OZARKS TO THE DELTA".
- Subtitle: "Cheese Dip, Barbecue & Catfish" (30).

## Icon (state-prompts/ICON-STYLE.md; all three pass check.swift; 60 px strip judged)
- **A, Nick's choice (2026-11-07): a bowl of cheese dip with a tortilla chip standing in it, on cardinal.** It reads at 60 px as dip and a
  chip, and cheese dip is the food Arkansas claims most loudly.
- B: two breaded fried dill pickle spears with green ends, on Ozark forest. Passes, but at 60 px they read as sausages.
- C: a South Arkansas pink tomato (the state fruit and vegetable) with a green calyx, on Ozark forest. Reads clearly, but says
  "farm stand" more than "restaurants". The best alternative.
Drawn by `scripts/icon-concepts.swift` (PNGs in `playbook/brand/`); `scripts/make-brand.swift` makes the app icon, site icons and
docs/og.png from A. scripts/make-site.py's LOGO is the same mark as an SVG.

## Palette (the prompt's: the Razorbacks and Arkansas State are both red, so a cardinal takes no side; no school marks; as built, Nick's yes 2026-11-07)
Ozark forest #2E5E3E (7.6:1 on white) for titles and tint; cardinal #9D2235 (white on it 7.7:1) for the hand-checked guide cards;
charcoal #2B2B2B for honors chips; diamond gold #E9C46A only as a fill under coal #1E1E1E, never as text; limestone #F3EEE3 pages;
a darker river teal #1F6672 (6.3:1) as an accent. Header band: a row of diamonds (the flag's diamond, the Crater of Diamonds) in
cardinal with white centres on an Ozark-forest line; no flag stars or text. Light mode only.
